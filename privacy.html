AI Studio Portable Recovery

Privacy information

Last updated: October 20, 2026. This describes a personal backup utility operated by its owner, not a service accepting other people's files.

Backup data and purpose

The owner selects development files, configurations and development-history archives for recovery. restic encrypts backup contents and filenames locally before rclone transfers repository data to the owner's Google Drive. The independent recovery password is kept separately from the repository.

Google access

The app uses Google's official authorization flow with the drive.file scope to manage specific files used with the app. Authorization tokens are stored separately from source backups on the owner's computer. General Drive access, Gmail and Google Docs permissions are not requested.

Storage and sharing

Encrypted backups remain in the owner's Drive account. They are not deliberately published, sold, shared with other users or sent to AI services by this workflow. Google handles storage and service metadata under its own policies. There are no advertisements or analytics in this utility.

Retention and control

The owner controls retention and deletion. The companion backup workflow does not automatically prune remote history. The owner can revoke authorization through Google Account's third-party connections controls and delete repository files through Drive. Losing both the recovery password and its separate recovery copy prevents decryption.

This information website

These static pages contain no backup files, account identifiers, credentials, tracking scripts, forms or cookies. The website host may process ordinary technical request metadata under its own privacy policy.
